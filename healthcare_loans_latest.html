Why this page exists separately. The most interesting healthcare borrowers in the loan market have no listed equity — Waystar, athenahealth, Radiology Partners, PointClickCare, Team Health, LifePoint. They can never appear on a page keyed to equity tickers, so this is the only place to look at healthcare loan pricing directly. It is also the debt that mid-cap healthcare actually issues: several coverage names have term loans and no bonds at all.
Discount margin, not yield to maturity. A term loan pays SOFR plus a spread, so a fixed-rate YTM would be invented — the reported coupon is only today's reset. DM is the spread over SOFR this mark implies if SOFR never moves, which is what loan desks quote. "Yield at reset" is shown beside it for a YTM-shaped number, but DM is the comparable one. And unlike bonds, a loan below par is credit or illiquidity by construction: loans are continuously callable at par, so a healthy borrower refinances rather than leaving its debt marked down.
