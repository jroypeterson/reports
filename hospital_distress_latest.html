Hospital distress — the full census

Every Medicare-participating hospital's annual cost report, FY2016–FY2025 · 5,520 hospitals in the latest complete year · generated 2026-08-01 02:40 UTC

Are more hospitals distressed than in the past?

No — fewer hospitals are losing money than before the pandemic. 28.6% of hospitals ran a negative total margin in FY2025, against 32.0% in FY2016 and a pre-pandemic norm around 32–35%.

The real event is FY2022, when 41.3% lost money as pandemic relief ran out and labour costs stayed high — and it hit nonprofits hardest, 47% of them in the red with a median margin of +0.6%. The sector has recovered every year since. That is the opposite of a slow bleed, and it matters for reading the bond screen: distressed hospital credits there are individual situations, not a sector in decline.

Government hospitals are the persistent exception — a median margin near +2.9% even in a good year, structurally thinner than either nonprofits or for-profits throughout.

What is measured. Total margin = net income ÷ total revenue, taken from Worksheet G-3 of each hospital's cost report. This is the all-in figure and is comparable to published benchmarks. It is not the "operating margin" Kaufman Hall and the AHA report — that excludes non-operating income and would not match. Every hospital is counted once per fiscal year, using its most complete filing, and years are the cost report's own fiscal year, which is staggered across the calendar rather than aligned to it.
Why this exists alongside the bond screen. The bond panel only ever sees hospitals whose debt some fund still holds — a small and self-selected slice that omits exactly the credits a fund has already sold. This is the whole census, from an entirely separate source, so the two agreeing is corroboration rather than an echo.
Excluded as incomplete: FY2014 (18), FY2015 (3,404), FY2026 (12). CMS names each archive for its processing year, not the fiscal years inside it, so the newest and oldest years of any ingest hold only part of the census. Charting them beside complete years would show a cliff that is an artefact of ingestion rather than anything about hospitals.
