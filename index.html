How the pieces fit. Two independent evidence bases, because neither can substitute for the other. The bond and loan pages price individual instruments from fund holdings filings — but they only ever see debt some fund still owns, which is a self-selected slice that omits exactly the credits a manager has already sold. The census covers every provider from cost reports, so it can say whether the sector is deteriorating at all. Individual distressed credits mean something quite different depending on which way the census is pointing.
Freshness. Bond and loan marks come from quarterly holdings filings and run roughly 60 days in arrears — they show where a bond is, not where you could deal. Cost reports refresh quarterly and are re-pulled automatically when they go stale. Everything regenerates Monday mornings; new debt filings are scanned weekly and posted to #credit.
