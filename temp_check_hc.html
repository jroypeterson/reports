Fiscal-period reconciliation

111 of 111 measured cells are calendar-aligned; 0 are not. Period basis across live rows — 11 measured (the filing states start and end dates), 4 point-in-time snapshots at a month end, and 29 assumed calendar (marked ~: earnings_kpi stores a fiscal quarter label with no dates, and managed-care filers are calendar filers, but this sheet says it is assuming that rather than implying it checked).

Every cell is placed by the mid-point of its reporting period, so a fiscal quarter running Nov–Jan lands in calendar Q4, not Q1. Fiscal year-end offset is not the same thing as period-boundary offset: McKesson (March year-end), Cencora (September) and Cardinal Health (June) all carry fiscal labels that differ from the calendar while their period dates line up exactly.
